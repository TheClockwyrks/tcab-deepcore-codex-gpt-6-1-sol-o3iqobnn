# Deepcore

The planet is dead. Your way home is not built yet. Take a drill and a battered jetpack into the dark, and turn the rock beneath your boots into an escape rocket.

Every expedition starts with a shaft. Follow a vein of Ferron, carve a side passage into richer ore, then decide whether there is room for one more find. Your cargo has weight. A heavy haul climbs slowly, and an overloaded jetpack cannot lift you at all. The descent costs little; the return is the gamble.

Back at camp, sell your minerals and choose what comes next: another tank of fuel, a stronger drill, a bigger hull, or one more piece of the rocket. Keep a teleporter for a desperate return and explosives for the boulders in your way. Save your progress at the pad before committing to another run.

Below the gray rock lie crystalline prizes, quiet gas pockets and molten seams. A scanner leads you toward Resonite and Cryenite. At the bottom waits the Core, and the final component demands a Sample that will not stay stable for long. Bring it home before the countdown ends. Choose a shorter expedition or a marathon descent, and decide whether death means returning to your last save—or losing your only chance.
